🇵🇷 Puerto Rico at the World Baseball Classic

Nobody has come so close so often without taking the trophy home: Puerto Rico finished runner-up in 2013 and 2017 with teams the whole Caribbean fell in love with. Their identity: gold-glove catchers and a bullpen that never blinks.

Puerto Rico debuted at the Classic in 2006 and appears in 6 of the six editions Invicto covers (2006, 2009, 2013, 2017, 2023, 2026): 184 roster records with position, club at the time and the game's 1-99 rating (methodology). Their historical peaks in the data: Edwin Díaz (2023, rating 89); Carlos Beltrán (2006, rating 87); Yadier Molina (2013, rating 86).
