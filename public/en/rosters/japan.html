🇯🇵 Japan at the World Baseball Classic

The giant of the tournament: champions in 2006, 2009 and 2023. The last title ended with the most famous scene in Classic history, Ohtani striking out Trout for the final out. No country has dominated the event like this.

Japan has played all six editions Invicto covers (2006 to 2026): 185 roster records with position, club at the time and the game's 1-99 rating (methodology). Their historical peaks in the data: Shohei Ohtani (2023, rating 95); Munetaka Murakami (2023, rating 88); Shinnosuke Abe (2013, rating 88).
