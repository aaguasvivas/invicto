🇧🇷 Brazil at the World Baseball Classic

Brazil qualified for the first time in 2013 with a generation formed between São Paulo and the minor league system, with a strong Japanese heritage. Paulo Orlando went on to win a World Series.

Brazil has played two of the six editions Invicto covers (2013, 2026): 58 roster records with position, club at the time and the game's 1-99 rating (methodology).
