🇩🇴 Dominican Republic at the World Baseball Classic

The republic of baseball. What happened in 2013 is the reason this game is called Invicto: the Dominican Republic won the whole tournament without losing a single game, the only perfect run in Classic history.

Dominican Republic debuted at the Classic in 2006 and appears in 6 of the six editions Invicto covers (2006, 2009, 2013, 2017, 2023, 2026): 180 roster records with position, club at the time and the game's 1-99 rating (methodology). Their historical peaks in the data: Francisco Liriano (2006, rating 96); Albert Pujols (2006, rating 93); Juan Soto (2023, rating 92).
