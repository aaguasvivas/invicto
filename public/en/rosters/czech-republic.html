🇨🇿 The Czech Republic at the World Baseball Classic

Czechia stole everyone’s heart in its 2023 debut: a roster of semi-professionals (firefighters, teachers, stockbrokers) that competed with total dignity against superstars. Eric Sogard gave them big league craft.

The Czech Republic has played two of the six editions Invicto covers (2023, 2026): 61 roster records with position, club at the time and the game's 1-99 rating (methodology). Their historical peaks in the data: Eric Sogard (2023, rating 61); Boris Večerka (2023, rating 50); Willie Escala (2023, rating 49).
